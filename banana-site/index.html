Quick Facts

🌍

Globally Loved

Over 100 billion bananas are eaten worldwide every year, making them one of the most consumed fruits on Earth.

🌿

Technically a Berry

Botanically speaking, bananas are berries — and the plants they grow on are giant herbs, not trees.

🧬

Mostly Cavendish

The vast majority of bananas exported around the world are a single variety: the Cavendish.

Nutrition

A medium banana (about 118g) is a tasty source of energy and key nutrients.
